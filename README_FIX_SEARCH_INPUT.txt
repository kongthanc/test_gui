CAP NHAT TIM KIEM, GOI Y SACH VA NHAP UNICODE

1. Tim kiem sach
- O tim kiem van chi tra cuu theo ma sach, ten sach va ten tac gia.
- Ket qua tim kiem duoc giu nguyen khi chon mot sach.
- Khu vuc goi y nam ben duoi va chi cap nhat khi chon sach khac.

2. Goi y sach
- Da chuyen viec lay sach goi y xuong book_service, duyet truc tiep danh sach sach.
- Uu tien sach cung tac gia, sau do bo sung sach cung the loai.
- Loai tru sach dang chon va khong them trung.
- Khong dung search_books(category) de loc the loai nua, vi search_books chi tim ma/tieu de/tac gia.
- Da kiem thu bang g++ voi Data/book.txt: chon B006 cho ra B018, B019 (cung tac gia), sau do B005, B020, B021 (cung the loai).

3. Nhap Unicode
- Nhan cac Unicode codepoint hop le tu Raylib, khong gioi han rieng vung Latin.
- Backspace xoa tron mot ky tu UTF-8.
- Bo sung Ctrl+V de dan van ban Unicode tu clipboard, giup co duong nhap du phong.
- Chua the xac nhan truc tiep UniKey/IME trong cua so Raylib tren Windows tu moi truong build nay.
- Neu UniKey van khong ghep dau: kiem tra UniKey dang o bang ma Unicode va thu go trong Notepad. Sau do thu Ctrl+V vao o nhap de phan biet loi IME voi loi xu ly clipboard.

Khong thay doi Data/user.txt, Data/book.txt hoac du lieu muon sach.
